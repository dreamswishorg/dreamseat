How to delete your account:

Option 1 (Inside the DreamEats App):

  • For Customers: Open the app → go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • For Merchants: Open the portal → go to Profile & Settings → Delete Account.

Option 2 (Web Request if App is Uninstalled):

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, submit the form below. Our support team will verify your identity and purge your account within 48 hours.

What happens to your data:

  • Permanently Deleted: Your profile details (name, email, phone number, address, avatar), authentication credentials, device push tokens, saved payment tokens, and notification preferences.
  • Retained Data: Past financial transaction and invoice records are retained strictly for the statutory period required under Ghana tax and accounting law (GRA compliance). These records are decoupled from your active login.
Deletion Request Received
We have received your account and data deletion request. A confirmation email and SMS will be sent to your registered contacts to finalize identity verification and initiate account purging.
