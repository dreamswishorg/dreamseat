Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • DreamEats Ghana

Overview: DreamEats is committed to safeguarding your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, protect, and process your information when you access our surplus food rescue application.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, browse food packages, or place reservations on DreamEats, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and optional profile picture.
  • Transaction Data: Details of orders placed, payment status, Mobile Money provider (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money), and pickup collection stamps. We do not store your Mobile Money PINs or full debit card numbers on our servers.
  • Location Data: Geolocation coordinates (with your permission) to calculate distance to nearby partner bakeries, restaurants, and grocery stores.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, device model, operating system, and push notification tokens for order updates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To process surplus food package reservations and coordinate pickups with local merchants.
  • To send instant order confirmation notifications and pickup window reminders.
  • To credit and track your DreamPoints rewards and CO₂ environmental savings.
  • To provide customer support and investigate refund requests.
  • To prevent fraudulent claims and ensure platform security.

3. Payment Security & Data Sharing

All financial transactions are processed securely through regulated payment gateways (Paystack) licensed by the Bank of Ghana. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.

4. Account Deletion & Data Rights

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and the Ghana Data Protection Commission principles, you retain full rights over your data:

  • Right to Deletion: You may permanently delete your account directly inside the app by navigating to Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete Account. All associated personal records, tokens, and saved preferences are purged permanently.
  • Right of Access & Correction: You may update your profile details at any time from your Profile settings.
